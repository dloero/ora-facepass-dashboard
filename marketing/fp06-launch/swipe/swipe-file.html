Read this first

The decision. Run the FP06 as a launch-list campaign, not a purchase campaign, until Jacky confirms three things: price, ship date, and where it will be sold. You cannot sell what you cannot price or ship. A reservation list is an asset Ora owns, and on launch day it can be pointed at orafacepass.com, at Lowe's, or at dealers. The creative, the landing page and the sequences are built so the only thing that changes at launch is the call to action.

Lane fit. The homeowner track is the Lowe's retail lane. A retail buyer wants to see sell-through support, and this kit is that support: proof of demand, a list to point at stores, and end-cap-ready visuals. The property manager track is the Colorado multifamily lane and drives to a 15-minute walkthrough with the 5-door minimum intact. Anything else (dealers, integrators, senior care) stays parked through November 15.

What you are testing. Which promise stops the scroll for a homeowner: solar, the package camera, the interior screen, face unlock, or privacy. Four concepts go live on day one. The rest rotate in on day three.

KPIs and the 14-day test

Working targets for the first 14 days. The homeowner numbers are assumptions for a consumer hardware waitlist and should be reset after 100 reservations. The PM numbers come from the internal paid-media framework.

$12Target cost per reservation (homeowner). Ceiling $20.
25%Target landing page reserve rate from paid traffic. Below 12% means the page, not the ad.
$35Target cost per PM lead. Ceiling $45 (framework).
12%PM lead to booked walkthrough. Below that, the qualifying questions are too loose.

Kill and scale rules

  • An ad with $60 spent and no reservation is paused. An ad above the ceiling after $120 is paused.
  • An ad under target with 10 or more reservations gets its budget doubled, once, then held for 72 hours.
  • Reserve rate under 12% for two days: fix the page before touching the ads. First suspects: hero load time, form friction, review banner left on.
  • Frequency above 3 in the retargeting set: swap creative before adding budget.
