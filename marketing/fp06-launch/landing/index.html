Questions

Straight answers.

Is my face stored in the cloud?

No. Your face is stored as an encrypted 3D model on the lock itself. It is never uploaded. No photos are kept, and you can delete your data at any time from the lock or the app.

Does it work in the dark?

Yes. Recognition uses infrared, not a normal camera, so it works in complete darkness. Strong direct sunlight on the sensor can slow it down. A covered entry or a hand shading the sensor fixes that.

What if the battery runs down?

The solar panel charges the batteries in daylight and the dual-battery design carries the lock through the night. If it ever runs all the way down, plug a power bank into the USB-C port on the bottom of the lock to get in.

What if the Wi-Fi goes down?

Every way in still works, including face unlock, because recognition happens on the lock. You will not get remote notifications or the live video call until the connection comes back.

Can I still use a regular key?

Yes. A physical keyway sits behind the doorbell button, and two keys come in the box. Fingerprint, code, card and the app all work too. Your face is one option, never the only one.

Do I need an electrician?

No. The FP06 installs on a standard door with a screwdriver, and an illustrated guide and video walk you through it. Professional installation is available if you would rather not.
